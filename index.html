Research profile

Autonomy for heterogeneous robotic teams at sea

My research combines robotics software, guidance and control, artificial intelligence, state estimation, and sensor fusion to support reliable operation across unmanned surface, aerial, and underwater vehicles.

I work across simulation, system integration, and field experimentation, with a particular interest in decentralized cooperative navigation for USV–UAV teams in the maritime domain.
