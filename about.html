ABOUT

Building autonomy that works beyond the lab.

I am a marine robotics and autonomous systems engineer pursuing a Ph.D. in Ocean Engineering at Florida Atlantic University.

My work sits at the intersection of robotics software, guidance and control, artificial intelligence, state estimation, sensor fusion, and real-world maritime experimentation. I develop systems in simulation, integrate them on physical platforms, and validate them in the field.

My broader research goal is to enable heterogeneous robotic teams to operate more intelligently, cooperatively, and reliably in complex ocean environments.
