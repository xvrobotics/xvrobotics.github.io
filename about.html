An ocean engineering researcher working at the intersection of autonomous systems, maritime robotics, and artificial intelligence.

Biography

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Ocean Engineering and a Graduate Research Assistant at Florida Atlantic University, specializing in autonomous maritime systems.

My research spans guidance, navigation and control; robotics software; artificial intelligence; state estimation; sensor fusion; and experimental marine robotics. I develop algorithms in simulation, integrate them on physical platforms, and evaluate their performance through field trials.

My doctoral research focuses on decentralized cooperative navigation for heterogeneous USV–UAV teams. The broader aim is to enable marine robots to operate more intelligently, cooperatively, and reliably in complex and uncertain ocean environments.

In 2026, I am also a Visiting Researcher at the NATO STO Centre for Maritime Research and Experimentation (CMRE) through its Visiting Research Programme.
